## ProcessInput: first mismatch and new evidence

At **00401500**, first +2b is the final-return displacement. First substantive difference remains **+6d2 / 00401bd2**, in event 30207: original loads each of fourteen screen addresses into EAX before pushing; candidate directly pushes the fourteen addresses. The other first five close-all expansions already have the original load/push form. The later campaign-cheat close-all correctly uses direct pushes. Missing address moves account for fourteen bytes.

The purchase expansion has another one-byte difference: original preserves pending cost in EDI and adds subtotal into EAX; candidate keeps pending in EAX and computes total with `lea edi,[ebx+eax]`. With subsequent table alignment, the extent remains **3360 versus 3376**. Its public purchase helper's **303-byte instruction body is equal**, but the full 501-byte extent owns ProcessInput's dispatch tables and is DIFF.

The new nested-default rule is reproduced in the actual full TU:

* Spell out all three SendEvent defaults in the purchase/cheat bodies: ProcessInput loses EBP, starts differing +4, and becomes 3352 bytes. Three accepted matches are lost; rejected.
* Keep those explicit arguments but omit only GetString's trailing default inside either BriefFormat call: EBP and the original 3360-byte baseline return. A default in an inner argument with outer format arguments already pending is sufficient here. `r3_nested_explicit_events_warning`, `_confirmation` and `_both` each retain 39, but do not repair the fourteen address loads.
* Keeping SendEvent defaults and changing either/both nested GetString spellings is neutral. Seven full-body controls are in `r3_nested_defaults.py`.
