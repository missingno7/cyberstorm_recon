## New compiler facts
- `if (!p)` versus `if (p == 0)` is NOT equivalent under -oxt -oe when the then-block contains an
  -oe-inlined call with a switch. The `==0` form places the then-block after the inline's case blocks
  (`je then; jmp after`). AnimBitmapFrame, both cases.
- The ASSERT with a literal 0 third argument is pushed through whichever register is known to be zero
  (AnimEnsureFX, AnimBitmapFrame case 1). A pushed register therefore does not by itself prove the capture
  form.
- Natural 3-level index loops over typed arrays under -oxt reproduce "pointer walk + byte offset +
  surviving IMULs" via strength reduction (AnimBitmapSweep). Explicit offset/pointer spellings do not.
