## logic/lactors.c

**00464054, module initializer.** Its sole call constructs 512 actors at 004dd184. The descriptor at 0048e0d8 has three relocated pointers. Offset +1 points to the default constructor at 00464d74, +5 to the undefined copy-constructor fallback at 0047e008, and +9 to the destructor at 00464d6c. The stride at +13 is 0xa8. Both game support bodies are five-byte return-this functions.

The slot lifetime change in Reserve is a reconstruction hypothesis. It explicitly destroys the previous slot and placement-constructs the replacement using the inline placement operator also used in shell/missbrf.c. Keeping the destructor implicit makes Watcom emit its callable body without adding static teardown registration. Reserve, CreateActor and every other previously matched main-code body retain their exact bytes. The initializer remains 24 bytes. The out-of-class constructor and external placement operator probes grew accepted code.

## logic/bestloc.c


- **00465f1c, BestLocDispatch → IntelFindBest, STRICT, 431 bytes.** The existing three-argument caller identifies this search entry. It checks the target, establishes the search bounds, chooses near/far/random tolerance, visits candidates, and clears the result if it is the current cell or fails the final negative-score comparison. The switch's parity arm loads the actor field before the turn value. The writable source path and assert line 154 also match.
- **00465d31, BestLocFindCandidate, independently identified, DIFF.** IntelFindBest passes this exact callback address at 0046606c. The promotion implies its symbol, plus bestLocLimit, bestLocLimitBase, bestLocTolerance, bestLocCompareMode and bestLocBestValue. On the live ledger the callback extent still prints UNPLACED because newly implied targets resolve caller fixups during that pass. Its own diagnostic gives 463 bytes and 111 differing bytes. The original executes 469 bytes through RET before the next switch's filler and table.
- The callback rejects every occupied cell, scores admitted cells using range and ray visibility, and breaks ties using the candidate's position or the angle. The two omitted RayOcclusion arguments restore the EBP frame. In the far branch the original explicitly scales the cell index by 12 and adds the full offset to the table pointer in EDI before loading height at +1. The candidate still folds the final scale into the load. At +6c the original copies the index to EAX and fetches the actor table into EBX. The candidate exchanges those homes. Terrain-grid member and height-accessor probes did not remove these differences.
